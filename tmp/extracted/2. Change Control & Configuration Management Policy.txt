1.  System  Change  Policy  
Purpose  and  Scope  
This  policy  deﬁnes  how  changes  to  information  systems  are  planned  and  
implemented.
 This  policy  applies  to  the  entire  information  security  program  at  Mindsmiths  (i.e.  to  all  
information
 
and

This  policy  deﬁnes  speciﬁc  requirements  to  ensure  that  changes  to  systems  and  
applications

reviewed,
 
approved,
 
communicated,
 
implemented,
 
documented,
 
and

greatest

of
 
success.
 
Where

not

provides

post-implementation

 2.  Any  changes  to  the  staﬀ  dedicated  to  any  project,  like  the  termination  or  transfer,

 3.  All  change  requests  must  be  documented.  4.  All  change  requests  must  be  prioritized  in  terms  of  beneﬁts,  urgency,  eﬀort

 5.  All  implemented  changes  must  be  communicated  to  relevant  users.  6.  Change  management  must  be  conducted  according  to  the  following  procedure:  a.  Planning:  plan  the  change,  including  the  implementation  design,  
scheduling,
 
and
