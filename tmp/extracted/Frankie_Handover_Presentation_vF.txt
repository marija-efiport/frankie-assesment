What Frankie Does: Beyond VEA's Scope
Capabilities VEA does not cover and the Novi Sad team will need to learn from scratch
Event Management
Full lifecycle: creation, WhatsApp invites, reminder sequences, waitlists, 
QR check-in, attendance tracking, post-event feedback
Student Preferences

menu subscriptions (daily/weekly), all via WhatsApp conversation
Conversation Analysis

satisfaction reports per intake; escalation triggers
Ticketing & Assignment
Tickets opened from flagged conversations and automatically assigned 
to the responsible KB manager based on knowledge area

--- PAGE 8 ---
Key Handover Risks
Service continuity depends on a planned, phased approach
CRITICAL
Loss of institutional knowledge: the Frankie team holds five years of accumulated understanding of student 
behaviour, platform edge cases, seasonal operational patterns and FS stakeholder dynamics. Much of this is not 
formally documented.
CRITICAL Dashboard outages with no support: the incoming FS development team will have no existing familiarity with the FS 
Dashboard codebase. Immediate handover without knowledge transfer leaves production bugs unsupported.
HIGH Knowledge base degradation: without trained KB managers, content becomes outdated during intake periods, 
causing Frankie to give students incorrect information about events, programmes and processes.
HIGH
Mindsmiths relationship breakdown: the current team has established working relationships and escalation paths 
with Mindsmiths. An abrupt transition without proper onboarding of the incoming team risks delayed response 
times and platform miscommunication.
