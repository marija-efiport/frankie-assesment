Appendix  A:  Handling  of  Classiﬁed  Information  Information  and  information  systems  must  be  handled  according  to  the  following  
guidelines*:
 1.  Paper  Documents  a.  Internal  Use  i.  Only  authorized  people  may  have  access.  ii.  If  sent  outside  Mindsmiths,  the  document  must  be  sent  as  
registered
 
mail.
 iii.  Documents  may  only  be  kept  in  rooms  without  public  access.  iv.  Documents  must  be  removed  expeditiously  from  printers  and  fax  
machines.
 b.  Restricted  i.  The  document  must  be  stored  in  a  locked  cabinet.  ii.  Documents  may  be  transferred  within  and  outside  Mindsmiths  only  
in
 
a
 
closed
 
envelope.
 iii.  If  sent  outside  Mindsmiths,  the  document  must  be  mailed  with  a  
return
 
receipt
 
service.
 iv.  Documents  must  immediately  be  removed  from  printers  and  fax  
machines.
 v.  Only  the  document  owner  may  copy  the  document.  vi.  Only  the  document  owner  may  destroy  the  document.  c.  Conﬁdential  i.  The  document  must  be  stored  in  a  locked  cabinet.  ii.  The  document  may  be  transferred  within  and  outside  Mindsmiths  
only
 
by
 
a
 
trustworthy
 
person
 
in
 
a
 
closed
 
and
 
sealed
 
envelope.
 iii.  Faxing  the  document  is  not  permitted.  iv.  The  document  may  be  printed  only  if  the  authorized  person  is

Data  Retention  Policy  
The  Purpose  of  Data  Retention  1.  This  policy  must  be  followed  whenever  Personal  Data  is  processed  for  or  on  
behalf
 
of
 
Mindsmiths.
 2.  The  General  Data  Protection  Regulation  (EU)  2016/679  (GDPR)  sets  out  speciﬁc  
requirements
 
regarding
 
the
 
retention
 
of
 
Personal
 
Data.
 
In
 
particular:
 a.  To  the  extent  that  the  data  records  of  Mindsmiths  (Data  Records)  contain  
Personal
 
Data,
 
Mindsmiths
 
must
 
comply
 
with
 
applicable
 
data
 
protection
 
laws,
 
including
 
(where
 
relevant)
 
the
 
GDPR.
 b.  The  GDPR  requires  Personal  Data  to  be  deleted  or  anonymized  when  they  
are
 
no
 
longer
 
needed
 
given
 
the
 
purposes
 
for
 
which
 
they
 
are
 
held.
 3.  The  purpose  of  this  Policy  is  to  ensure  that:  a.  Data  Records  are  adequately  protected  and  maintained;  b.  Data  Records  containing  Personal  Data,  which  are  no  longer  required  are  
discarded
 
at
 
the
 
appropriate
 
time;
 c.  Mindsmiths's  data  retention  principles  will  help  Mindsmiths  to  ensure  the  
exercise
 
of
 
individuals'
 
data
 
protection
 
rights.
 4.  Capitalized  terms  not  deﬁned  directly  in  this  Policy  have  the  meaning  assigned  
to

in

connected
 
to
 
this

in

of
 
the

 Guiding  Data  Retention  Principles  1.  These  are  Mindsmiths'  guiding  data  retention  principles:  a.  Fairness :  All  Processing  of  Personal  Data  must  be  fair,  proportionate,  and
