1.  Policy  Statement  a.  Protecting  access  to  IT  systems  and  applications  is  critical  to  maintain  the  
integrity
 
of
 
the
 
Mindsmiths
 
technology
 
and
 
data
 
and
 
prevent
 
unauthorised
 
access
 
to
 
such
 
resources.
 b.  Access  to  Mindsmiths  systems  must  be  restricted  to  only  authorized  users  
or
 
processes,
 
based
 
on
 
the
 
principle
 
of
 
strict
 
need
 
to
 
know
 
and
 
least
 
privilege
  
2.  Background  a.  Access  controls  are  necessary  to  ensure  only  authorized  users  can  obtain  
access
 
to
 
a
 
company's
  
information
 
and
 
systems.
 b.  Access  controls  manage  the  admittance  of  users  to  system  and  network  
resources
 
by
 
granting
 
users
 
access
 
only
 
to
 
the
 
speciﬁc
 
resources
 
they
 
require
 
to
 
complete
 
their
 
job
 
related
 
duties.
  
3.  General  Requirements  a.  Mindsmiths  will  provide  access  privileges  to  technology  (including  
networks,
 
systems,
 
applications,
 
computers
 
and
 
mobile
 
devices)
 
based
 
on
 
the
 
following
 
principles:
 i.  Need  to  know  –  users  or  resources  will  be  granted  access  to  
systems
 
that
 
are
 
necessary
 
to
 
fulﬁll
 
their
 
roles
 
and
 
responsibilities.
 ii.  Least  privilege  –  users  or  resources  will  be  provided  with  the  
minimum
 
privileges
 
necessary
 
to
 
fulﬁll
 
their
 
roles
 
and
 
responsibilities.
 b.  Requests  for  users’  accounts  and  access  privileges  must  be  formally  
documented
 
and
 
appropriately
 
approved.
 c.  Requests  for  special  accounts  and  privileges  (such  as  vendor  accounts,  
application
 
and
 
service
 
accounts,
 
system
 
administration
 
accounts,
 
shared
 
/
 
generic
 
accounts,
 
test
 
accounts
 
and
 
remote
 
access)
 
must
 
be
 
formally
 
documented
 
and
 
approved
 
by
 
the
 
system
 
owner.
 d.  Application  and  service  accounts  must  only  be  used  by  application  
components
 
requiring
 
authentication;
 
access
 
to
 
the
 
passwords
 
must
 
be
 
restricted
 
to
 
authorized
 
IT
 
administrators
 
or
 
application
 
developers
 
only.
 e.  Where  possible,  Mindsmiths  will  set  user  accounts  to  automatically  expire  
at
 
a
 
pre-set
 
date.

5.  Shared  User  Accounts  a.  Where  possible,  the  use  of  speciﬁc  network  domain  “security  groups”

 b.  Shared  user  accounts  are  only  to  be  used  on  an  exception  basis  with  the  
appropriate
 
approval.
 
This
 
includes
 
general
 
user
 
accounts
 
such
 
as
 
“guest”
 
and
 
“functional”
 
accounts.
 c.  When  shared  accounts  are  required:  i.  Passwords  will  be  stored  and  handled  in  accordance  with  the  
Password
 
Policy.
 ii.  The  use  of  shared  accounts  will  be  monitored  where  possible,  
including
 
the
 
recording
 
of
 
the
 
time
 
of
 
access,
 
the
 
reason
 
for
 
accessing

user

When

user

privileges,
 
such

is

and

to
 
the

logs
 
must

and

a.  Where  possible,  all  default  user  accounts  will  be  disabled  or  changed.  
These
 
accounts
 
include
 
“guest”,
 
“temp”,
 
“admin”,
 
“Administrator”,

7.  Test  Accounts  a.  Test  accounts  can  only  be  created  if  they  are  justiﬁed  by  the  relevant

or
 
project
 
team
 
and

by

 b.  Test  accounts  must  have  an  expiry  date  (maximum  of  6  months).  
Maintaining

 c.  Test  accounts  will  be  disabled  /  deleted  when  they  are  no  longer  
necessary

8.  Contractors  and  Vendors

contractors
 
/

In

Mindsmiths
 
systems

 b.  Mindsmiths  will  maintain  a  current  list  of  external  contractors  or  vendors

access
 
to
 
Mindsmiths
 
systems.
  
9.  Access  Control  Requirements  a.  All  users  must  use  a  unique  ID  to  access  Mindsmiths  systems  and  
applications.
 
Passwords
 
must
 
be

in
 
accordance
 
with
 
the
 
Password
 
Policy.
 b.  Alternative  authentication  mechanisms  that  do  not  rely  on  a  unique  ID  and  
password
 
must
 
be
 
formally
 
approved.
 c.  Remote  access  to  Mindsmiths  systems  and  applications  must  use  
two-factor
 
authentication
 
where
 
possible.
 d.  System  and  application  sessions  must  automatically  lock  after  10  minutes  
of
 
inactivity.
