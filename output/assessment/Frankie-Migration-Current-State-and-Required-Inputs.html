How we complete the assessment

Each step produces evidence for the migration decision

StepWhat we doWhat we can conclude
1. Receive and mapLink functions, repos, services, databases, integrations and ownersWhat transfers and what remains external
2. Reproduce and testBuild applications, deploy in staging and exercise key workflowsWhat works and which changes are required
3. Rehearse data transferRestore agreed data; reconcile records, relationships and attachmentsWhether the proposed transfer is complete and repeatable
4. Size and recommendDefine work packages, dependencies, acceptance and recovery criteriaEffort ranges, responsibilities and a supportable migration plan

A reliable migration estimate follows these checks. No delivery date or headcount is established by the current documents.

Proposed assessment approach based on the FS framework. Execution and production migration are subsequent activities. | 8 / 10
