# What people ask Frankie

**Scope:** 427,081 message records, 16 April 2025–13 October 2026.

- **93,987 incoming messages — 22.01%** of all records.
- **333,094 outgoing messages and templates — 77.99%.**
- Of incoming messages, **58,449 (62.19%)** were classified as questions or requests. The remaining **35,538 (37.81%)** include replies, greetings, reactions and unclear statements.

## What replies people receive

The requests form **42,444 conversation segments**. Each segment is counted once below.

| Reply pattern | Segments | Percentage |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Reply containing a visible link | 8,078 | 19.03% |
| Reply without a visible link | 32,992 | 77.73% |
| No eligible reply within the segment | 1,374 | 3.24% |
| **Total** | **42,444** | **100.00%** |

Additional signals: **5,186 segments (12.22%)** contain uncertainty or missing-information wording; **6,308 (14.86%)** contain staff-referral/contact wording. These overlap and do not automatically mean a bad answer.

A link may accompany a full answer. “No eligible reply” uses a 30-minute inactivity window and excludes staff messages and unrelated templates.

## Good and bad answers

**Only the existing 40-case content review has quality judgments; these percentages do not apply to all conversations.**

| Reviewed result | Cases | Percentage |
| --- | ---: | ---: |

Direct answers still require factual verification. Useful examples included menus and IT opening hours; failures included repeating exam links instead of giving dates and sending onboarding after an opt-out request. **An overall correct/incorrect-answer percentage is not established.**

## Main event use

**127,810 event-related templates = 77.81% of all 164,256 templates.** This includes Opening Week logistics. Invitations and announcements alone account for **97,975 templates (59.65%)**. The main measured event activity is promoting events, especially careers and networking; these counts do not measure attendance.

Figures are rounded to two decimals. Counts were checked against the complete message export.
